Consumer Mailing Lists

Consumer mailing lists help you reach households based on the characteristics that matter to your campaign. From broad consumer audiences to highly targeted demographic segments, we'll help you build the right prospect list.

Available consumer selections

Consumer lists can be developed using available demographic, geographic, household, lifestyle, and purchasing attributes. Common selections include age and date of birth, income, homeowner status, gender, marital status, presence of children, home value, length of residence, lifestyle, interests, purchasing characteristics, geography, phone, and email.

Who uses consumer mailing lists

Insurance agents, home service companies, nonprofits, retailers, healthcare marketers, financial professionals, and local businesses use consumer lists to find new customers in a defined market. The right list depends on the offer, the geography, and the channel you plan to use.

How targeting works

There is no single best consumer mailing list. A homeowner list for a roofing company is different from a senior list for a Medicare campaign or a donor-style audience for a nonprofit. Tell us who your best customers are and we'll research the available data.
