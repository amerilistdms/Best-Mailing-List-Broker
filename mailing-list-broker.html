What a Mailing List Broker Does

With thousands of potential data sources, databases, list owners, compilers, and specialty audiences available, finding the right marketing list isn't always as simple as buying names online. A mailing list broker acts as your data sourcing and audience targeting partner.

More than names

Anyone can sell you names. An experienced list broker helps you determine which data makes the most sense for your campaign: audience, targeting criteria, database, available fields, permitted usage, and quantity.

How we work

Tell us what you are trying to accomplish. We'll help define the target audience, research available mailing lists, identify demographic selections, compare data options, run list counts, determine contact channels, provide pricing, and prepare data for marketing.
