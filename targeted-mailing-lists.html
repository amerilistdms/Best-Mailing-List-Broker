Targeted Mailing Lists

Targeted mailing lists start with the audience, not a generic file of names. We help you match available consumer, business, and specialty data to your offer, your market, and the way you plan to market.

Start with the customer, not the database

You do not need to know the name of a database or exactly which selects to request. Tell us who your best customers are, where they are located, what you are selling, and how you will contact them. We'll research the options.

Geographic targeting

Lists can be built by ZIP Code, city, county, state, radius, SCF, DMA, custom territory, or nationwide. Local businesses and national marketers can use the same process with different geography.

Counts before you buy

A free list count shows how many prospects match your criteria before you purchase. That is the practical way to compare targeting options and understand cost.
