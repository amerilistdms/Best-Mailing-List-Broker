    What is a mailing list broker?

    A mailing list broker helps businesses identify, source, and purchase marketing lists from available data sources. A good list broker does more than provide names. The broker helps determine the appropriate audience, targeting criteria, database, available fields, permitted usage, and quantity for a marketing campaign.

    Can I buy a targeted mailing list?

    Yes. Mailing lists can be customized using available geographic, demographic, business, lifestyle, and other marketing criteria. The exact selections depend upon the database and audience.

    Can you provide consumer mailing lists?

    Yes. Consumer prospect lists can be developed using available demographic, geographic, household, lifestyle, and other targeting attributes.

    Can you provide B2B mailing lists?

    Yes. Business mailing lists can target companies and decision makers by industry, geography, company characteristics, job title, executive function, and other available criteria.

    Can you provide phone numbers?

    Yes, telephone information is available for many consumer and business audiences. Availability and appropriate usage depend upon the particular database and campaign.

    Can you provide email addresses?

    Email data and email append services are available for many audiences. The appropriate product depends upon your target market and intended use.

    Can you help me determine which list I need?

    Absolutely. You don’t need to know the name of a database or exactly which selections to request. Tell us what you’re selling and who you want to reach. We’ll help with the rest.
